## 3. Speedup and parallel efficiency

`metrics_shared.py` does NOT compute these (verified by grep — no such symbol).
Definitions adopted from Shashi at `83a1512`:

* `speedup_blast_over_sequential` = blast **chunks_per_s** / sequential **chunks_per_s**, same arm, same corpus (`metrics.py:53-59`, fed chunks_per_s at `:133-134`). His words: "ratio of ratios — immune to the 'you gave one side more workers' objection because each side is normalized against itself."
* `parallel_efficiency` = speedup / concurrency (`metrics.py:62-66`); the divisor is `RR_THREADS` for the engine arm and `HS_WORKERS` for the framework arm (`bench.py:805,1013-1016`). 1.0 = perfect linear scaling.

**CHUNKS per second, not docs** — a blast-docs/s-only figure cannot produce this number; it needs the SEQUENTIAL leg's chunks_per_s from the same corpus.
